On the desktop

Pinned, the widget is part of the desktop: it stays exactly where it is, cannot be dragged or resized, sits behind whatever you are working in, and is still there after “show desktop”. To move it, unpin it — here, with the pin button on the widget itself, or from the icon by the clock — drag it where you want it, and pin it again. Double-click a pinned widget to open the panel.

Pinned, the widget stays where it is, cannot be dragged and is not held above your windows. To move it, unpin it with the pin button on the widget itself, drag it, and pin it again. Double-click a pinned widget to open the panel. Closing the windows keeps Daybook running for the widget; to bring a hidden widget back, start Daybook again from the app menu, and to stop it, press Quit Daybook.
